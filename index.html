    Apple

    Apple is a fruit. It is usually red, green, or yellow. It is sweet and crunchy.
    Apples contain vitamins, fiber, and water. They are good for our health.
    We can eat apples fresh or use them to make juice, cakes, and salads.

    Orange

    An orange is a fruit. It is usually orange in color.
    It tastes sweet and sometimes sour. Oranges contain vitamin C,
    which helps support our immune system.
    We can eat oranges fresh or make orange juice.
